# Style study: BSS 6, 7, 8 (women's luxury watch, 2026-11-23)

Source: `C:\Users\pc\Desktop\inspiration\BSS 6 A3`, `BSS 7 A4`, `BSS 8 A4` (preview files).

## Format
- **4:5** (1024×1280), 30 fps, **23–27 s**.
- About **9–10 shots** per ad: a new shot every **1.5–3 s** (scene cuts BSS 7: 1.9, 3.7, 5.1, 7.0, 7.5, 11.5, 19.5, 22.5 s).

## Pictures
- Close-ups of hands with long manicured nails, the watch held to the camera, put on the wrist, taken out of a jewelry box or gift box (roses), sparkle flashes on the crystals.
- Warm, cosy settings: café table, jewelry-shop counter, red velvet with plants and a butterfly stand.
- BSS 6 opens with 3 smiling women showing their watches (social proof / "bestie" angle).

## Voice and script
- One **continuous female voice-over** from start to end, warm and persuasive, no pauses between parts. Music very quiet under it.
- About **60–70 words** for 23–27 s.
- Structure:
  - **Hook (0–3 s)**: a question or a gift problem. "Struggling to find the perfect gift that will genuinely leave her speechless?" / "Looking for a special gift that will truly impress her?" / "Get one for yourself, one for your bestie…"
  - **Body**: "Skip / forget ordinary gifts, surprise her with this stunning watch" → product details (crystal-encrusted bracelet, gold-tone finish, fine-jewelry look) → emotion (she'll fall in love, feel special every time she wears it) → occasions (birthdays, anniversaries, just to show her how much she means to you).
  - **CTA (last 3–4 s)**: "Tap the link below now and make her day" / urgency in BSS 6 ("this exclusive bundle is limited and stocks are running out").
- For our COD markets the CTA becomes: order now, pay cash on delivery.

## Captions
- **Word-by-word karaoke**: 2–4 words on screen at a time, changing with the voice.
- Rounded heavy font, white with a dark shadow; **one key word in bright yellow and bigger** (gift, speechless, crystal, luxurious, finish, birthdays…).
- Small and low: centred at about **77 %** of the height.
- No banner, no logo.

## End
- Last 3 s: **red outlined arrows pointing down** (to the button under the ad) while the voice says "tap the link below".

## What the platform needs to match it
1. 4:5 output (1080×1350) next to 9:16.
2. Montage mode: the body made of several short shots (≈2 s each) taken from all ticked body clips, not one long clip.

4. One continuous voice for the whole script.
5. Scripts written in this gift/emotion structure (60–70 words), CTA in COD form.
